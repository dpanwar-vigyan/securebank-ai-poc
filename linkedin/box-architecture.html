Which lane?

Most banks need both. Box AI answers questions about the document in front of you; the external lane answers questions about everything you hold.

The question looks like…LaneWhy
"Does this voucher match the disputed transaction?"Lane 1 · Box AIOne case, a few files, answered inside the dispute workflow
"Pull the applicant's income and employer from this mortgage application"Lane 1 · Box AI ExtractStructured fields from one document, written back to Box metadata
"What are the change-of-control terms in this deal?"Lane 1 · Box AIDeep reading of one large document; check Box AI's size limits for very long deals
"Which customers raised similar complaints last year?"Lane 2 · vector indexSearch across thousands of documents, not one
"How many disputes did each branch get?"Lane 2 · metadata indexCounts and trends are SQL, not reading
"Combine statement history with CRM notes"Lane 2Data outside Box can't reach Box AI
"Must run on our approved model with our guardrails"Lane 2The bank controls the model, prompts and logging end to end
