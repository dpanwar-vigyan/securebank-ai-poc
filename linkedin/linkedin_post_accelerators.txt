Banks want AI at the frontline. Fast.
But any shortcut through a core system poses a multitude of risks on regulated processes.

The answer isn't to slow down — it's to decouple.

I've built a set of production-ready AI accelerators for regulated industries that sit completely outside your core systems and connect to them safely through an Ethical Wall — APIs, events and webhooks only. No core system is ever modified.

Here's what's running in the demo 👇

💬 AI Document Q&A — bankers ask questions in plain English, answers grounded in their own documents. Powered by AWS Bedrock.

⚙️ Intelligent Document Pipeline — PDF lands in S3, Orkes Conductor orchestrates the full DAG: classify → extract → chunk → embed → store. Every step is a serverless Lambda. Fully observable, fully resumable.

🧑‍💼 Human-in-the-Loop Decisions — banker approves, escalates or reopens a case. Temporal workflows hold state for days waiting on a human signal. The AI resumes exactly where it left off.

The architecture principle is simple:
→ Core systems (Temenos, Appian, ServiceNow, Salesforce) stay frozen
→ AI infrastructure moves fast on the outside
→ Orkes and Temporal stitch it together durably

This isn't just a banking story. Any regulated industry — insurance, healthcare, government, legal — faces the same challenge. Move fast on AI without breaking the systems you can't afford to touch.

These accelerators are production-ready. I'm looking to partner with organisations ready to move.

📩 Contact me to confidently bring AI to life in your regulated industry — without touching the systems you can't afford to break.
